Who We Are

Your direct bridge to Indonesia's finest agricultural products

PT. Citra Minindo Persada is a Jakarta-based general trading company specialising in connecting global buyers with the finest Indonesian agricultural commodities — from the volcanic highlands of Sumatra to the spice islands of Maluku.

We work directly with trusted farmers, cooperatives, and producers across the Indonesian archipelago, ensuring consistent quality, full traceability, and competitive pricing for every order — with complete trade documentation handled in-house.

Our team manages every step: sourcing, quality inspection, documentation, customs clearance, and freight coordination — so you receive your goods with confidence.

What We Offer

Complete Export Trade Services

Every service you need to source Indonesian products and receive them at your destination — managed from our Jakarta office.

Product Sourcing

Export Management
